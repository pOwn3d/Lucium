Lucium is a web browser for iPhone and iPad. Every web page is shown with WKWebView (WebKit); the app has no other web engine (guideline 2.5.6).

SIGN-IN AND TESTING
There is no account, sign-in, purchase, subscription or advertising in the app, so no demo account is needed. Open the app: a start page and a bar at the bottom appear. Tap the address field in the bar, type a web address or a search, and the page opens. Nothing else has to be set up.

WHERE TO FIND EACH FEATURE
- Tabs: the right-hand button of the bottom bar opens the tabs sheet (pinned tabs, folders, tabs of today, archive, favourites). "Privacy", "Lock" and "Licenses" are the last rows of that sheet.
- Spaces and profiles: the round buttons in the bottom bar are the Spaces; touch and hold one for "Spaces and profiles", where a Space and a profile are created. Two profiles keep separate cookies and website data.
- Private tab: in the same menu ("New private tab"). Nothing is kept once it is closed.
- Command bar: the address field. It searches open tabs and favourites and offers commands.
- Clear the data of one site: touch and hold the address field.
- Privacy settings: tabs sheet, "Privacy": protection level, HTTPS first, Global Privacy Control signal, removal of tracking parameters.
- Lock: tabs sheet, "Lock": a Space or the whole app can be locked with Face ID, Touch ID or the device passcode (LocalAuthentication; the app receives only a yes or a no and never a biometric).
- Licenses and credits: tabs sheet, "Licenses".

THE CONTENT BLOCKER
The blocker uses WKContentRuleList. The filter lists (public lists of ad and tracker rules) are converted when the app is built and are inside the app: nothing is downloaded, and they change only with an app update. Their authors and licenses are listed in the app (Licenses).

JAVASCRIPT THE APP RUNS IN PAGES (guidelines 2.5.2 and 4.7)
The app adds its own scripts to pages with WKUserScript, to hide cookie-consent banners and to apply the blocking rules of its lists and its YouTube rules. All of this code is in the app bundle: no script, rule or other code is downloaded, and no native function of the device is exposed to a web page (no script message handler).

NETWORK
The app makes no connection of its own: no analytics, no crash reporting, no account, no synchronization, no update check, no download of lists. It connects only to the pages the person opens, and loads what those pages load.

APP TRANSPORT SECURITY
Info.plist sets NSAllowsArbitraryLoadsInWebContent. A browser opens the pages the person asks for, and some are http. With "HTTPS first" the app asks for https:// first and opens the http:// page only if the site refuses the connection or stays silent for 5 seconds, and says so. A page with an invalid certificate is never opened over http without the person's tap. NSAllowsArbitraryLoads is not set.

PRIVACY AND ENCRYPTION
PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy declares no tracking, no data collected and no tracking domain, and none of the required-reason APIs is called. The privacy label answers "Data Not Collected". The only encryption is what iOS provides for HTTPS (WebKit), and Face ID is used only through LocalAuthentication.

OTHER POINTS
- No donation or payment button in the app (guideline 3.2.2).
- The default-browser entitlement is not requested in this version.
- YouTube loads and plays; the app applies rules of its own against its ads, and the blocking there is partial. Videos protected by DRM may not play.
- Lucium is free software under the GPL-3.0-or-later with an additional permission that lets the maintainer distribute it through the App Store; the source code is public at https://lucium.app/en/.
