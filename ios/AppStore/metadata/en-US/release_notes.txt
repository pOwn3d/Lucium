The first version of Lucium for iPhone and iPad.

• Spaces, each with its own tabs, pinned tabs and folders, and profiles that share no cookie
• A bottom bar within reach of your thumb, a tabs sheet and a command bar
• A built-in blocker for ads, trackers and cookie banners
• HTTPS first, the Global Privacy Control signal, tracking parameters removed
• Private tabs, and clearing the data of a single site
• A lock with Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode, for a Space or the whole app
